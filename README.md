# Online Recipe Sharing Platform

A web application designed for food enthusiasts to discover, share, and save their favorite recipes from around the world.

---

## Features

- **User Authentication:** Secure sign-up, login, and profile management.
- **Recipe Creation & Editing:** Easily upload recipes complete with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, prep/cook times, and photos.
- **Search & Filter:** Find recipes by category, ingredients, or dietary preferences.
- **Favorites & Collections:** Save recipes to your personal cookbook for quick access later.
- **Community Interaction:** Rate recipes and leave reviews or comments.
